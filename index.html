Why now

Contracts are multiplying. Senior auditors aren't.

Deployment volume keeps climbing while deep-review capacity hasn't kept pace and audit windows shrink. That gap is measured in coverage, cost, and time — and it's exactly where AI earns its place, without ever replacing human judgment.

The coverage gap

Manual review samples the risky paths it has time for. Machine analysis attempts all of them — so the long tail stops hiding.

The speed gap

First-pass triage that took days drops to hours, moving experts to the hard problems sooner within a fixed engagement.

The trust gap

The real blocker isn't capacity — it's "can I trust an AI audit?" We answer it with transparency, PoCs, and a human sign-off.

Objections, answered

The questions institutional buyers actually ask

Can I trust an AI audit? +
The AI never has the last word. It surfaces and reproduces candidates; a named human auditor triages every one, and only reproduced, human-confirmed findings reach your report. You're buying human judgment with machine reach — not a black box.
What about false positives? +
We don't hide them behind a dashboard. Two gates protect you before anything reaches your report: human triage removes noise, and a mandatory PoC means anything unreproducible is dropped. You see confirmed, runnable issues — not a wall of maybes.
Does a working PoC mean it's exploitable in production? +
Not automatically — and we say so. A PoC proves the bug is real and reproducible; a human auditor then judges whether it's economically and operationally exploitable in your live environment, and grades severity accordingly. Reproducibility removes hallucinations; human judgment sets the real-world stakes.
What if you miss a critical vulnerability? +
No audit — human or AI — can promise zero misses, and we won't pretend otherwise. What we give you is a written scope with explicit exclusions, a coverage manifest showing exactly what was reviewed, and a fix-verification re-audit. You know precisely what we checked and what we didn't.
What can the AI not catch? +
Novel economic and incentive exploits, off-chain trust assumptions, and governance-process risk still depend on human auditors. We state the limits explicitly in every engagement — honesty about misses is part of the trust.
Do humans still do the audit? +
Yes. AI is leverage, not a replacement. It expands coverage and speed so our auditors spend their time on the hard, protocol-specific problems — and a person signs the final report.
Which models and tools do you use? +
Engagements include a methodology appendix: the analysis techniques, tooling, and model roles used on your codebase. Institutional buyers don't buy black boxes, so we don't ship one.
